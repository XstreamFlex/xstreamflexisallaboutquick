01 Disruptive Privacy Philosophy

Xstreamflex is founded on the principles of complete digital democratization, simplicity, and total autonomy. Unlike closed-off web platforms or drag-and-drop systems that lock your visitors’ telemetry inside proprietary "black boxes", Xstreamflex ensures you retain total control of all source configurations.

Our flagship project, Xsite, delivers fully realized HTML/CSS layouts straight to your storage systems or private code repositories. We believe that what is built on your computer or configured by our rapid generator is yours—and yours alone.

"Our mission is designed with absolute love for the least capable, least knowledgeable, and least experienced user to find rapid success without compromising their intellectual property or visitor integrity."

02 Information We Collect

We prioritize simplicity. Because we are designed to facilitate immediate launches with minimal technical hurdles, we gather only the minimum telemetry necessary to support automated services:

  • Account Credentials: Basic contact details (such as names, emails, and phone numbers) needed for authentication and portal setup.
  • Brain Dump Configurations: The core inputs, company summaries, styles, goals, and content suggestions you provide to customize your Xsite template layouts.
  • System Diagnostics: Server error logs and platform diagnostics to ensure our visual layouts display reliably for users of all technical and cognitive levels.

03 Code Ownership & GitHub Autonomy

Traditional builders hide your code behind subscription walls, retaining backend configurations and templates on their platform. Xstreamflex takes the exact opposite approach.

The Traditional Builder way:

They control your servers, obscure your code, demand recurring fees to stay live, and limit your ability to migrate your site structure elsewhere.

The Xstreamflex Blueprint:

We request tokenized access to push layouts to your custom GitHub repository. You possess total code ownership and can host it wherever you like.

To deploy code on your behalf, we leverage highly secured GitHub OAuth tokens. We access only the designated public or private repository assigned for your deployment. We never read your other personal codebases, credentials, or private keys.

04 Stripe & Integrations

Our expansion roadmap includes features that hook your sites up to key external modules, like Stripe for checkout and external email responders for automations.

Once you follow our clear configuration tutorials to connect custom Stripe portals, Xstreamflex acts purely as an infrastructure coordinator. Your customer's payment details, billing flows, and secure transaction data are routed directly through your dedicated payment provider—we never capture, store, or sell checkout metrics.

05 Cookies & Tracking Control

Xstreamflex uses functional, non-persistent cookies exclusively to preserve your workspace layout, active developer settings, and authentication states. We do not integrate third-party data tracking matrices, cross-context marketing engines, or invasive demographic mapping software on your live, deployed sites.

06 Accessibility & Inclusivity Policy

Part of protecting our users is making sure our system remains fully accessible. Our platforms are designed with care to ensure individuals with cognitive, visual, or physical differences can operate our tools and access our legal documentation with ease.

Whether you are a senior citizen, a youngster starting a first project, or using helper devices to browse, our clean, standard layout protocols ensure you are never locked out of your digital rights.

07 Data Retention & Portability

You can demand the deletion of your account and layout assets at any time. When you request a removal, we clear your OAuth access tokens and deployment configurations from our platform database.

Because you own the live pages on your personal GitHub and web hosting system, your live websites remain fully yours to maintain even if you close your account with Xstreamflex.
