Operational Engine Spec

Version 2.4 (Optimized for Freedom)
01

Data Ownership & Control

In Plain English: You are launching your business with Xsite, and when your project takes off, you own everything. There is no "black box" where we hold your code hostage. Everything we build from your brain dump belongs to you, and we store nothing that would prevent you from moving it to any system, server, or hosting framework you desire in the future.

💡 Grandma-Friendly Tip: If you build a digital lemonade stand here, it's yours to keep. You can take your code blocks and pack them up in a suitcase anytime you want.

Under our strict product architectural frameworks, Xstreamflex asserts zero proprietary rights or intellectual property restrictions on the compiled, deployed code output generated via Xsite. Once code is generated or deployed to a remote user repository (including but not limited to GitHub platforms), full licensing, ownership, and liability shift exclusively to the user account holder. Xstreamflex does not retain backup records of compiled static repositories unless explicitly mandated for local session caching.

02

How Your Email & Brain Dump is Handled

In Plain English: To register, you only need to give us an email address. No passwords if you prefer, just clean and simple access. When you give Xsite your "brain dump" (ideas, target audience, preferred colors, copy styles), we use it to dynamically render your landing page. We don't sell this brain dump to third-party ad networks, and we don't read your business ideas to steal them.

Information collection is limited to essential operational parameters. Account creation requires a single authenticated electronic mail address (Email). Brain dump data (defined as text inputs, style preferences, corporate branding instructions, and target market heuristics provided to our generation endpoint) is compiled exclusively to structure and write standard-compliant HTML, CSS, and Markdown payloads. We do not aggregate user business models for external resale, telemetry extraction, or predictive analytics modeling.

03

GitHub & Stripe Connectors

In Plain English: To make launching effortless, Xsite helps you connect your custom domain, hook up Stripe, or save directly to a GitHub account. We only request the exact minimum permissions needed to push files. We don’t snoop around your other projects or access your private repositories. Your financial relationships with Stripe remain secure and independent.

Xsite utilizes secure OAuth authentication protocols for GitHub. This deployment pathway permits temporary token allocation exclusively configured to generate directories, push compiled landing page packages, and establish root DNS mapping parameters. Financial transactions for paid subscription tiers (Free, Starter $5, Pro $9, Business $29, Lifetime $199) are integrated via tokenized gateways on Stripe. Credit card credentials, security codes, and transactional tokens are managed by Stripe and are entirely isolated from the Xstreamflex persistent storage apparatus.

04

The "Absolute Erasure" Switch

In Plain English: If you decide to close up shop, delete your pages, or take your files completely off Xsite, we believe "delete" should mean *delete*. We have designed an ultra-simple one-click erasure. No loops to jump through, no calling support lines. You click delete, and your record of the brain dump is instantly wiped clean.

Pursuant to international data regulations (including GDPR and CCPA), users retain full sovereignty over their personally identifiable information. Xstreamflex implements a structural single-action termination feature. Initiating this action immediately flags and purges physical records of the account, active pages, cached code bases, metadata attributes, and connected DNS structures from our main transactional database shards within 24 hours of confirmation.
